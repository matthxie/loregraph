Emmanuel Nuhu Kure, also known as Apostle (Dr.) Emmanuel Nuhu Kure is Nigerian Gospel minister and televangelist - featuring on Dove TV. He is the founder and president of the prayer and Christian missionary oriented ministry, The Throneroom (Trust) Ministry, having branches within Nigeria and abroad, including the United States of America, Cameroon, Niger, Indonesia and the United Kingdom, and headquarters in Kafanchan, Kaduna State, Nigeria. He was the National Secretary of the Pentecostal Fellowship of Nigeria (PFN).
