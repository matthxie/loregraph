Family
In 1860 Morrison married Mabel, a daughter of the Rev. R. S. C. Chermside, rector of Wilton, Wiltshire. His wife survived him with two sons, Hugh and James Archibald, who were both Members of Parliament, and two daughters.
A daughter Dorothy married Stafford Harry Northcote, Viscount Saint Cyres. Another daughter married Stephen Herbert Gatty.
