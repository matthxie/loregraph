Crystallography
Jolliffeite has cubic symmetry and is therefore isometric meaning crystallographically, it contains three perpendicular axes of equal lengths.  It has four three-fold axes all inclined at the same angle to the crystallographic axes.  Optically, Jolliffeite is isotropic.  Isotropic minerals have a single refractive index and are not birefringent.  The single refractive index of Jolliffeite can be determined by its relief.
