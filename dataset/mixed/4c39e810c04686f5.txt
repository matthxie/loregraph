The Collection is a box set by American rock band Toto. It consists of the band's first seven albums on CD, and a DVD of Greatest Hits Live and More (released in 1992 on VHS under the title Toto Live, then on DVD in 2002), a concert recorded at Le Zénith, Paris, on October 1990.
